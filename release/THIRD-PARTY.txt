Third-party components in the Release zips of samwu07/xegfx-thinkbook14plus-mtl
==============================================================================

These zips are a backup of one working Lenovo ThinkBook 14+ 2024 (Core Ultra 7 155H, iGPU 8086:7D55, macOS 15.8.1).
They are offered for non-commercial use only (several parts are PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0), as is, with no
warranty. Every licence named below is in LICENSES/ next to this file. "Source" is where the corresponding source is;
for the GPL, LGPL and APSL parts that is the source this binary was built from, or the upstream release it came from.
Credit goes to the authors below; this repository only adds the patches in metal-stack/ and the scripts.
